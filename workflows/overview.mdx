## Overview

A workflow runs on its own. It starts when something happens, such as a file being uploaded or an email attachment arriving, or on a schedule. It then checks the file and does something with it, such as moving it to the right folder, renaming it or asking someone to approve.

Workflows replace the old automatic filing on upload. Nothing is filed automatically unless a workflow does it.

## Who can use workflows

- **Owners and admins** create, edit, run, pause and delete workflows.
- **Members** can open any workflow to see its steps and settings, see its activity, and decide approvals that are addressed to them.

## Open workflows

Open **Settings** > **Workflows** and click **Open workflows**. The Workflows page has three tabs:

- **Workflows**: every workflow in the workspace, with whether it's **Active** or **Paused** and when it last ran
- **Approvals**: decisions waiting on you, and the ones already made
- **Activity**: every run, filterable by **Needs attention**, **In progress** and **Completed**

## Create a workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new workflow">
    On the Workflows page, click **New workflow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe it">
    Under **What would you like to automate?**, describe it in a sentence, for example "When I get email attachments, sort invoices into client folders". The builder assembles the steps on a canvas. To build it step by step yourself instead, click **Build manually instead** and drag steps from **Nodes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check and adjust">
    Click any step to change its settings. Click **Validate** to check the workflow for problems.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and turn it on">
    Click **Save**, and set the switch to **Active**. To try it on a few real files first, click **Run now**, which runs it on up to 5 files.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also ask [the agent](/agent/overview) to set one up, for example "Whenever a file lands here, file it by client and year." It shows you a draft that you can **Open in editor** or **Save**.

## Triggers: what starts a workflow

| Trigger | Starts when |
|---------|-------------|
| **File Uploaded** | A file is added to The Drive AI or to a connected storage account |
| **On a Schedule** | A set time comes round, for example every weekday at 9:00 |
| **Email Attachment** | An attachment arrives in a connected Gmail or Outlook account |
| **Slack** / **Microsoft Teams** | A file is shared in a connected channel |

Watching a connected storage account, email or messaging app needs that account connected in **Settings** > **Integrations**, on a plan that includes connected accounts. Workflows on files uploaded to The Drive AI work on every plan.

### Schedules

An **On a Schedule** trigger goes through a folder in The Drive AI, or in a connected account, on a repeating schedule:

- **Repeat**: **Hourly**, **Daily**, **Weekly** or **Monthly**, at a time in your time zone
- **Each run goes through**: **New and changed files only**, or **Every file, every run** (use this for rules based on a file's age)

## Steps

**Decisions** send each file down a path:

- **Check File Details**: by type, age, size, name or location
- **Ask AI About the File**: AI reads the file and answers a question, like "Is this a signed contract?"
- **Ask a Person to Approve**: pauses until someone decides

**Actions** do something with the file:

- **Move** and **Rename**: to a folder or name you set, or one the AI works out from the content
- **Read Details from File**: pulls out values like client, date or amount so later steps can use them in names and folders
- **Merge Files** and **Split Document**
- **Save a Copy**: into The Drive AI or a connected account
- **Move to Trash**: recoverable
- **Send a Message**: to Slack or an email address

## Approvals

An **Ask a Person to Approve** step pauses the run and shows what would happen. The request appears under **Approvals** on the Workflows page, and a banner there counts the decisions waiting on you.

- Click **Approve** or **Reject**. Batches of files can be decided together with **Approve all** or **Reject all**.
- Add a note to redirect the step, for example "this belongs to the Smith matter, not Jones".
- An approval that isn't decided in time expires, and the workflow follows its expired path.

The person who sets up the step chooses who may decide: admins, specific people, or everyone.

## Limits

Each plan includes a number of workflow files per month. When a workspace uses them all, the Workflows page says **Monthly file limit reached**, and the owner or an admin can click **Get more files**. See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-pricing).

## Coming from Auto Organization

If your workspace used automatic filing before, an owner or admin can open **Settings** > **Auto Organization**. Any organizing instructions you wrote are kept there, and one click turns them into a workflow: **Build a workflow from these instructions**, or **Build the workflow for me** if you never wrote any.
