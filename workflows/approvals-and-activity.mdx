## Overview

Every time a workflow handles a file, it records a run: which file, which steps it went through, and what each one did. Runs are listed under **Activity**. Runs that stop for a person's decision are listed under **Approvals**.

Both are tabs on the Workflows page. Click **Workflows** in the left sidebar to open it.

| Who | Can |
|-----|-----|
| Everyone in the workspace | See activity, decide approvals addressed to them, and answer requests to use their own account |
| Owners and admins | Also retry failed runs, and decide any approval |

## Activity

The **Activity** tab lists every run in the workspace, newest first.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workflows-activity.png" alt="The Activity tab with status filters, the workflow picker and file name search" />
</Frame>

Narrow the list with:

- **All**, **Needs attention**, **In progress** or **Completed**
- The workflow list, which starts on **All workflows**
- **Search file names**

Click **Load older activity** at the bottom for more. While runs are in progress the list updates by itself; otherwise click **Refresh**.

To see one workflow's runs, click **Activity** on its row in the **Workflows** tab, or open the workflow and choose **Activity** in the editor's left panel.

### What the filters mean

| Filter | Shows |
|--------|-------|
| **Needs attention** | Runs that failed, and runs waiting for a person to approve |
| **In progress** | Runs queued or running, including AI steps finishing in the background |
| **Completed** | Runs that finished, including ones that were skipped |

A skipped run isn't an error. It means the workflow looked at the file and had nothing to do, for example the copy was already saved there, the sender is blocked, or the workspace was out of [workflow files](/workflows/workflow-files).

On the **Workflows** tab, each workflow's row shows how its last run went: **Success**, **Failed**, **Awaiting approval** or **In progress**.

### Read a run

Open a run to see each step in order, with what it did in plain words, for example "Classified as "invoice" (96%)", "Moved to Clients/Acme/2026" or "Saved a copy to Inbox in Google Drive". A step's line links to the file where it can.

Click **Watch this replay on the canvas** to watch the file move through the workflow's steps, in the shape the workflow had when it ran.

## Retry a failed run

A failed run says which step failed and why, for example "The connection needs to be reauthorized" or "The file could not be found". Owners and admins see a button under it:

- **Retry**: for temporary problems. Click it to try again.
- **Fix & retry**: something has to change first, such as the step's settings, a reconnected account, or a file restored. Make the change, then click it.

A retry picks up from the step that failed, on the same file. It appears under the original run.

## Approvals

When a workflow reaches an **Ask a Person to Approve** step, it pauses that file and asks. See [Ask a Person to Approve](/workflows/steps#ask-a-person-to-approve) to set one up.

You hear about it three ways: an email, an **Approval needed** notification in the app, and a banner on the Workflows page, such as **1 approval is waiting on you**.

### Decide an approval

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Approvals">
    On the Workflows page, click the **Approvals** tab, or **Review** on the banner. The tab shows a count when something is waiting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check what will happen">
    Each card shows the workflow, the file, the question, and the steps that will run if you approve, for example "→ Move to Clients/Acme/2026". The file name links to the file. The time left shows on the card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a correction (optional)">
    Click **Add a correction** and type a note, for example "this belongs to the Smith matter, not Jones". Your note takes priority over what the AI would have decided on its own. If the card lists planned steps, approving with a note works them out again with your note in charge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide">
    Click **Approve** or **Reject**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* Screenshot: An approval card showing the planned actions, the time left, Add a correction, and the Approve and Reject buttons */}

Approving runs the planned steps on the file. Rejecting stops the run and leaves the file where it is, unless the workflow sends rejected files somewhere, which the card tells you.

A note steers the steps that take instructions, like **Move**, **Rename** and **Read Details from File**. On a step like **Move to Trash**, it's only kept for the record.

You can also approve or reject from a run in **Activity**.

### Decide many files at once

When several files are waiting on the same workflow's question, for example after a scheduled run went through a folder, they're grouped on one card. Click **Show the N files** to see them, and **Add a correction for all** for a shared note.

Click **Approve all N** or **Reject all**. For more than 10 files you're asked to click again to confirm, for example **Yes — approve all 24 files**. The decision covers exactly the files on the card. A file that arrives afterwards waits for its own decision.

You can still decide files in a group one at a time.

### Who can decide

The approval step chooses who is asked: admins, specific people, or everyone. Any one of them can decide. Owners and admins can decide any approval. If an approval isn't yours to decide, its card says "Someone else was asked to decide this one."

### Expiry

Each approval waits for the time set on its step: 24 hours, 3 days or 1 week. Approvals with less than 24 hours left are marked as expiring soon, the banner turns amber, and the tab's count turns amber too.

When an approval expires, the workflow follows its **Expired** path. If nothing is connected there, the file is left where it is.

### Decisions already made

Under **Decided** on the **Approvals** tab, choose a period to see every decision made in it: **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days**, **Last year** or **All time**. Click **Export CSV** to download what's shown, with when each was decided, the outcome, who decided, whether it was automatic, the file, the workflow, the actions and any note.

Decisions made automatically by an approval step set to **Only when unsure** are listed here too.

## Workflows that ask to use your account

Some workflows watch each member's own inbox or drive, for example "every member's Gmail" or "every member's Google Drive". The admin who builds one chooses who is asked. Nothing runs through your account until you agree, and you can leave at any time.

When you're asked, you get an **A workflow is asking to use your account** notification, and the Workflows page shows **1 workflow is asking to use your account**. Click **Review**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the request">
    Under **Your accounts in workflows** on the **Approvals** tab, the card names the workflow and what it watches, for example "Runs on attachments that arrive in your Gmail inbox." Click **See what it does** to open the workflow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the account">
    If you have more than one account that fits, choose which to use. If you have none, connect one in **Settings** > **Integrations**, then come back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join or decline">
    Click **Join**, or **Join with all N** for several accounts. Click **Decline** to say no.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Workflows you've joined stay listed under **Your accounts in workflows**. Click **Leave** to stop, or **Join** to rejoin one you left or declined.

Only you can join with your account. Admins see who has joined in the source's settings, but can't join for you.

## When an account is disconnected

A workflow that uses a connected account is paused when that account is disconnected, and when the workspace's plan stops including connected accounts. Owners and admins get an email and a **Workflows paused** notification listing the workflows.

Reconnecting the account doesn't turn the workflows back on, because the reconnected account may be a different one. Owners and admins are reminded that the workflows are still paused. To resume one, open it and switch it to **Active**. If the account still isn't connected, you'll see **Cannot resume yet** and which account to reconnect.

A workflow is also paused when the folder it watches can no longer be reached, or when the account it uses leaves the Teams team it watches.

For a workflow that watches each member's own account, only that member's part pauses. It shows as **Paused — account disconnected** under **Who has joined**, and the rest keeps running.
