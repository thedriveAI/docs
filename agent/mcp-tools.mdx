## Overview

The [agent](/agent/overview) can work in other apps, not only in your files. Connect an app and you can ask for things like "find the roadmap in my Notion" or "put a summary of this contract in my Clients database".

- **You connect your own account.** The agent works in the app as you and sees only what you can see there. Nobody else in the workspace uses your account, and you don't use theirs.
- **It reaches for an app only when you ask about it.** A request about your files doesn't touch your other apps.
- **Tools that only read**, like a search, run without asking.
- **Tools that can change something** in the app ask you first, every time, and show exactly what would be sent.

Apps are connected through MCP (Model Context Protocol), a standard way for AI assistants to use a service's tools. You don't need to know anything about it to connect an app from the list.

<Note>
  This page is about the agent using other apps. To use your The Drive AI files from Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and other AI tools, see [MCP Server](/integrations/mcp-server). Google Drive, OneDrive, SharePoint, Dropbox and Box aren't apps here: connect them as [storage](/agent/connected-drives), where the agent can search them, file into them and undo its changes.
</Note>

## Connect an app

Anyone in the workspace can do this. You don't need an admin.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Apps">
    Open **Settings** > **Integrations** and scroll to **Apps**, below **Email and messaging** and **Cloud storage**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    Click **Connect** on the app's row, for example **Notion**. A sign-in window opens. If nothing opens, allow pop-ups for The Drive AI and click **Connect** again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and choose what to share">
    Sign in to the app with your own account and approve what it asks. Some apps, Notion among them, let you choose which pages to share.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask the agent">
    The window closes and the row says **Connected with your account**. Ask the agent for something in the app, in your own words.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also start from a conversation. Ask the agent for something in an app you haven't connected, and it shows a card, such as **Connect Notion**, that opens **Settings** > **Integrations**. Connect there, then ask again.

Notion is on the list today. More apps are added as each is tested end to end. An owner or admin can [add any other app](#add-another-app) that has an MCP server.

## How the agent uses an app

You don't need to name a tool. Ask for what you want:

```text
Find my notes about the Henderson renewal in Notion and list the open questions.
```

```text
Summarise this contract into a new page in my Notion Clients database.
```

**Read-only tools run without asking.** The app marks which of its tools only read. The agent uses those freely, like searching your pages.

**Every other tool asks first.** The agent shows a card labelled with the app, for example **On Notion**. It says the action "Acts on Notion, outside your files, and cannot be undone here," and lists each value it would send. Click **Allow** to run it, or **Not now** to skip it.

These actions happen in the other app, so **Undo** in The Drive AI can't reverse them. To reverse one, do it in that app.

See [Approving and undoing](/agent/approving-and-undoing#actions-on-other-services).

### See what the agent can do in an app

On a connected app's row, click the tool count, such as **77 tools**. The list opens in two groups:

- **Runs on its own**: tools that look things up and change nothing.
- **Asks you first**: tools that change something in the app.

The list appears once someone in the workspace has connected the app.

## What each row says

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Not connected** | You haven't connected your account. Click **Connect**. |
| **Connected with your account** | Working. The agent can use the app as you. |
| **Your sign-in expired** | The app stopped accepting your sign-in. Click **Reconnect**. |
| **Shared by the workspace** · N tools | An app an admin set up with a key that everyone uses. |
| **Unreachable** · reason | The last check couldn't connect to the app's server. |
| **Off for this workspace** | An admin turned the app off. Only owners and admins see these rows. |

To stop the agent using your account in an app, click **Disconnect** on its row. This removes your sign-in from The Drive AI. To also remove The Drive AI from the app's own list of connected services, do that in the app's settings.

## For owners and admins

### Turn an app off or on

Every app's row has a switch. Off means nobody in the workspace can connect or use it, and members no longer see the row. You can turn an app off before anyone has connected it. Turning it back on restores each person's own connection.

### Add another app

You can add any app that has an MCP server reachable on the public internet over `https`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dialog">
    Under the **Apps** list, click **Add another app by its MCP server**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and enter the address">
    In **Name**, type the app's name, such as `Linear`. The agent's tools from it are named after it, for example `linear_search`. Paste the server's address into **Server URL**, for example `https://mcp.linear.app/mcp`. The app's own documentation lists it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how people connect">
    In **How people connect**, choose one:

    - **Each person signs in**, for an app whose server asks you to sign in in a browser. This is the most common, and the safest: everyone uses their own account.
    - **No authentication**, for a server that doesn't need a key.
    - **Bearer token (shared)**, then paste the token.
    - **API key in a header (shared)**, then enter the **Header name** the app expects (`X-API-Key` by default) and paste the key.

    Leave **Transport** on **Streamable HTTP (recommended)** unless the app says to use **Server-sent events (older servers)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    With **Each person signs in**, click **Save and sign in**: the app's sign-in window opens for your own account, and every other member gets a **Connect** button on the row. With the other choices, click **Add and test**: The Drive AI connects and tells you how many tools the agent gets, or why it couldn't reach the server.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A workspace can have up to 20 apps.

<Warning>
  A **shared** token or key is used for everyone in the workspace. Whatever that account can do in the app, every member's agent can do through it, after they allow each action that changes something. Prefer **Each person signs in** where the app supports it, and otherwise use a token with only the access your team needs. A shared token or key is stored encrypted and never shown again.
</Warning>

### Manage an app you added

- **Test** it. Click **Test** to connect again and see the tools it lists. An app people sign in to is tested with your own account.
- **Change it.** Click the app's name, change the details, and save. To keep the current token or key, leave the secret field blank. If you change the address or how people connect, everyone who had connected needs to connect again. If you rename an app, its tools keep their original names.
- **Remove it.** Click the trash icon and confirm. The agent no longer has its tools, and everyone's sign-in to it is forgotten.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Nothing happens when I click Connect" icon="window-restore">
    Your browser blocked the sign-in window. Allow pop-ups for The Drive AI, then click **Connect** again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The agent says I haven't connected the app" icon="plug">
    Each person connects their own account. A colleague having connected an app doesn't connect it for you. Open **Settings** > **Integrations** > **Apps** and click **Connect**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says my sign-in expired" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    The app stopped accepting the sign-in, for example because you removed The Drive AI in the app's settings. Click **Reconnect**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The agent can't find something I can see in the app" icon="magnifying-glass">
    It sees what you shared when you connected. In Notion, for example, you choose the pages. Disconnect and connect again to choose more, or share the page with the connection in the app.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says Google Drive (or another drive) is built in" icon="hard-drive">
    Drives aren't added as apps. Connect them under **Settings** > **Integrations** > **Cloud storage**, where the agent can search them, file into them and undo its changes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says the server URL must use https, or must be reachable on the public internet" icon="lock">
    Use the app's public `https://` address. Plain `http`, `localhost` and private network addresses aren't accepted, and the URL can't contain a username or password.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An app I added shows Unreachable" icon="plug-circle-xmark">
    Check the address, the transport and the token or key with the app, then click **Test**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A tool is slow or its answer is cut short" icon="hourglass">
    A tool has 60 seconds to answer. Very long answers are shortened before the agent reads them. Ask for something narrower, such as one page instead of all of them.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
