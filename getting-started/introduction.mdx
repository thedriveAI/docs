## What is The Drive AI?

[The Drive AI](https://thedrive.ai) is a workspace for your files, with an AI agent built in. Store files in The Drive AI, or connect the Google Drive, OneDrive, SharePoint or Dropbox you already use. Then ask the agent, in plain language, to find, read, organize or create files. Every change it makes is shown to you first or as it happens, and you can undo it. If part of a change can't be undone, the agent tells you.

For work that repeats, such as filing every invoice that arrives by email, you set up a workflow once and it runs on its own.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Bring your files">
    Upload files to The Drive AI, or connect your Google Drive, OneDrive, SharePoint or Dropbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask the agent">
    Open the agent and describe what you want, for example "Find the contracts that renew this quarter" or "Organize the invoices by vendor and year."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and approve">
    Small changes apply straight away. Larger ones wait for your OK. You can undo either from the chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Automate the repeat work">
    When you want something to happen every time, like filing every incoming invoice, an owner or admin sets up a workflow.
  </Step>
</Steps>
