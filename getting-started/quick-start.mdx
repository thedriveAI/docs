This page gets you from no account to a working setup in a few minutes. Each step links to a page with the full detail.

## 1. Create your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to The Drive AI">
    Visit [thedrive.ai](https://thedrive.ai) in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Click **Continue with Google**, **Continue with Microsoft** or **Continue with Apple**. Or enter your name, email and a password, and click **Create account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer a few questions">
    A short set of screens follows. You can click **Skip** at any point. If you answer **With my team** to **Who will use this?**, the app opens **Create Team Workspace** when you finish.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You now have a **personal workspace** that only you use. See [Profile and sign-in](/account/profile-and-sign-in) for more on accounts.

## 2. Create a team workspace (optional)

To work with other people, create a team workspace. It's separate from your personal workspace, which stays as it is.

1. Click your workspace name at the top of the left sidebar, then click **Create team workspace**.
2. Enter a name, choose the number of **Seats** and **Annual** or **Monthly**, and click **Create Workspace**.
3. Enter your payment details at checkout.

Team plans start with a free trial. See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-pricing#create-a-team-workspace), then [Members and roles](/account/members-and-roles) to invite people.

## 3. Add your files

You can upload files to The Drive AI, connect a drive you already use, or both.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Upload">
    - Drag files or folders from your computer onto the file browser, or
    - Click **New** in the toolbar and choose **Upload**.

    See [Uploading files](/features/uploading-files).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Connect a drive">
    On a plan that includes connected drives, open **Settings** > **Integrations** and, under **Cloud storage**, connect **Google Drive**, **OneDrive**, **SharePoint** or **Dropbox**. The agent can then work in that drive directly, and it appears under **Connected** in the left sidebar.

    See [Connected drives](/agent/connected-drives).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## 4. Give the agent its first task

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the agent">
    Open a folder, then click **Agent** in the toolbar, or press `⌘J` (Mac) or `Ctrl+J` (Windows).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for something">
    Try one of these:

    ```text
    What is in here, and what looks out of place?
    ```

    ```text
    Create a folder called Reports and move all the PDFs into it
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the change">
    If the agent proposes a change, it shows a card listing exactly what will happen. Small changes apply straight away. For larger ones, click **Apply N changes** or **Not now**. Once a change is applied, click **Undo** on its card to reverse it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If the panel says **The agent is not enabled for this workspace**, the workspace owner or an admin turns it on in **Settings** > **Agent** with **Available in this workspace**.
</Note>

<Tip>
  The agent works on the folder you have open. Open a folder first to keep the agent focused on it.
</Tip>

See [The agent](/agent/overview) and [Approving and undoing](/agent/approving-and-undoing).

## 5. Set up your first workflow

Owners and admins can set up workflows. Members can view them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Workflows">
    Click **Workflows** in the left sidebar, then **New workflow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe it">
    Under **What would you like to automate?**, describe it in a sentence, for example "When a PDF is uploaded, file it into a folder by client". The builder assembles the steps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and turn it on">
    Check the steps, click **Save**, and set the switch to **Active**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Workflows](/workflows/overview) and [Building with the agent](/workflows/building-with-the-agent).
