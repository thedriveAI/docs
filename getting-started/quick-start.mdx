## Create your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to The Drive AI">
    Visit [thedrive.ai](https://thedrive.ai) in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Sign in with **Google**, **Microsoft** or **Apple**, or create an account with your email and a password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the onboarding">
    Your personal workspace is created for you, and you can start adding files straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add your files

You can upload files to The Drive AI, connect a drive you already use, or both.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Upload">
    - Drag files or folders from your computer onto the file browser, or
    - Click **New** in the toolbar and choose **Upload**, then choose files or a folder and click **Upload**.

    See [Uploading files](/features/uploading-files).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Connect a drive">
    On a plan that includes connected drives, open **Settings** > **Integrations** and connect **Google Drive**, **OneDrive**, **SharePoint** or **Dropbox**. The agent can then work in that drive directly.

    See [Connected drives](/agent/connected-drives).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Give the agent its first task

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the agent">
    Click **Agent** in the toolbar, or press `⌘J` (Mac) or `Ctrl+J` (Windows).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for something">
    Try one of these:

    ```text
    What is in here, and what looks out of place?
    ```

    ```text
    Create a folder called Reports and move all the PDFs into it
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the change">
    If the agent proposes a change, it shows a card listing exactly what will happen. Small changes apply straight away. For larger ones, click **Apply** or **Not now**. Either way you can click **Undo** afterwards.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If the panel says **The agent is not enabled for this workspace**, the workspace owner or an admin turns it on in **Settings** > **Agent** with **Available in this workspace**.
</Note>

<Tip>
  The agent works on the folder you have open. Open a folder first to keep the agent focused on it.
</Tip>
