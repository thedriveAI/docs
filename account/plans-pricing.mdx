## Workspaces and plans

Every plan belongs to a workspace.

- **Personal workspace:** you get one automatically when you sign up. Only you use it, and it stays personal: it can't be turned into a team workspace.
- **Team workspace:** for working with other people, billed per seat. Team plans include everything in Max for every member, plus admin controls and one bill for the whole team. Business and Enterprise are arranged with our team.

## A few things worth knowing

- **Assistant tasks.** A task is a request the assistant acts on. Every plan includes a monthly number, pooled across the workspace on team plans: see the table above. They come back on your billing date, or on Free, on the day of the month the workspace was created. See [The agent](/agent/overview#tasks-and-limits).
- **Connected drives and accounts.** Google Drive, OneDrive, SharePoint, Dropbox, Gmail, Outlook, Slack and Microsoft Teams connections come with Max, Team, Business and Enterprise.
- **Workflow files.** Each plan includes a number of files that workflows can process each month. Owners can buy more when a workspace runs out.
- **Members don't see prices.** In a team workspace, only the owner sees billing. Members see what the plan includes, not what it costs.

## Upgrade

**Personal workspace**

1. Open **Settings** and choose **Billing**.
2. Next to **Plan**, click **View plans** and choose a plan.

**Team workspace**

A team plan doesn't upgrade your personal workspace. You create a separate team workspace, and your personal workspace stays as it is.

1. Click your workspace name at the top of the left sidebar, then click **Create team workspace**.
2. Enter a name, choose monthly or annual billing, and click **Create Workspace**.
3. Enter your payment details at checkout. Team plans start with a free trial (see the table above), once per person, and you're charged when it ends. The workspace is created when checkout is complete, and you're its owner.
4. Invite your team. See [Members and roles](/account/members-and-roles).

## Manage a subscription

Only the workspace owner can see and change billing. Open **Settings** > **Billing** to see the plan, its status, the renewal date and the billing email.

- **Seats (team workspaces):** shows how many seats are in use. Change the number with the **+** and **−** buttons. If you're paying for seats nobody uses, click **Release** to drop them.
- **Payment method, invoices and cancellation (team workspaces):** next to **Manage subscription**, click **Manage**.

Owners, admins and members can see usage, such as **Assistant tasks this month** and workflow files, in **Settings** > **Usage**.
