## Overview

A team workspace can have many members, and each one has a role. A personal workspace has only you, so it has no members to manage.

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
|------|------------------|
| **Owner** | Everything, including billing, changing roles, transferring ownership and deleting the workspace. Each workspace has one owner. |
| **Admin** | Manage members, workspace settings, integrations and workflows, and turn the agent on or off. Admins can't see billing, delete the workspace, or change the owner. |
| **Member** | Create, edit and delete files, use the agent, connect their own storage and email accounts, create file requests and signature requests, and view workflows. |
| **Viewer** | View files. Can't make changes. |
| **Guest** | Limited access to specific content that's been shared with them. |

A few details:

- Viewers and guests can't create file requests or signature requests, and they can't open workspace settings.
- Anyone except the owner can leave a workspace. To leave, ask the [agent](/agent/overview), or ask an owner or admin to remove you.
- Only the owner or an admin can connect Slack or Microsoft Teams. Any member can connect their own Google Drive, OneDrive, SharePoint, Dropbox, Gmail or Outlook account, if the plan includes connected accounts.
- Owners, admins and members can see which accounts are connected and who connected them. Only the person who connected an account can browse it or import from it.

## Invite people

Owners and admins can invite people.

1. Open **Settings** and choose **Members**.
2. Type an email in **Invite by email**, pick a role, and click **Invite**. If the workspace has no free seats, the owner's button reads **Add seat & invite**, and a seat is added to the bill. Admins can't add seats; they ask the owner.
3. To invite several people at once, click **Invite multiple people at once**.

Admins can invite members, viewers and guests. Only the owner can invite or promote admins.

## Change a role or remove someone

In **Settings** > **Members**:

- To change someone's role, pick a new role next to their name.
- To remove someone, click **Remove**. The owner can't be removed.
- To hand the workspace to someone else, the owner clicks **Make owner**. The previous owner becomes an admin.
