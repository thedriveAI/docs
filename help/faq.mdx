## General

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is The Drive AI?" icon="circle-question">
    The Drive AI is a workspace for your files with an AI agent built in. You can store files in The Drive AI or connect Google Drive, OneDrive, SharePoint or Dropbox. Then ask the agent, in plain language, to find, read, organize or create files. See [Introduction](/getting-started/introduction).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How is it different from Google Drive or Dropbox?" icon="scale-balanced">
    Google Drive and Dropbox store files. The Drive AI reads them. You can ask questions about what's inside your files and reorganize folders by describing what you want. You can also set up workflows that file new documents as they arrive. It can also work inside your Google Drive, OneDrive, SharePoint or Dropbox, so you don't have to move your files. See [Connected drives](/agent/connected-drives).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What file types are supported?" icon="file">
    You can upload any file. The Drive AI reads PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets, presentations and text files on every plan. Reading images, audio and video (with transcription) needs a plan that includes every file type — see [Plans and pricing](/account/plans-pricing) — with an active subscription.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my data secure?" icon="shield-halved">
    Files are encrypted at rest and in transit, uploads are scanned for malware, and your data is never used to train AI models. See [Security & Privacy](/features/security).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a mobile app?" icon="mobile">
    Yes, for [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-drive-ai-file-organizer/id6758524851) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bigyankarki.thedriveai). You can use it to scan documents, browse files and use the agent.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The agent

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What can the agent do?" icon="robot">
    It can find, read, move, rename, copy and trash files, create folders, and create documents, spreadsheets and presentations. It can work across hundreds of files at once. With your approval it can also share files, invite members, create file requests, prepare signature requests and draft workflows. See [The agent](/agent/overview).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where did Chat and Agent go?" icon="toggle-on">
    There is only one assistant now: the agent. It answers questions and it makes changes, so there's nothing to switch.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I undo what the agent did?" icon="rotate-left">
    Yes, in almost every case. Every change appears as a card in the chat. Click **Undo** on the card, or ask the agent to undo it. Files moved to the trash in The Drive AI can also be restored from **Trash**. On a work or school OneDrive or SharePoint, a file moved to the recycle bin may have to be restored there; the card tells you. See [Undo on each drive](/agent/connected-drives#undo-on-each-drive).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why does the agent ask before some changes?" icon="hand">
    Small changes apply straight away. Bigger changes, anything that moves files to the trash, and changes outside the folder you're in ask for your OK first. Owners and admins can change these thresholds in **Settings** > **Agent** under **Limits and approvals**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How many tasks can I run?" icon="gauge">
    A task is a request the assistant acts on, like "total my Acme invoices" or "rename these by date". Answering its questions or saying hello doesn't count. Every plan includes a monthly number: 20 on Free, 500 on Premium, 2,000 on Max, 2,000 per seat on Team and 4,000 per seat on Business, shared across the workspace. Free reads up to 50 files per task. They come back each month, and you can upgrade for more at any time. Check your usage in **Settings** > **Usage**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can the agent work in my Google Drive?" icon="google-drive">
    Yes, on plans that include connected drives: Max, Team, Business and Enterprise. Connect it in **Settings** > **Integrations**. The same goes for OneDrive, SharePoint and Dropbox. See [Connected drives](/agent/connected-drives).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can the agent move files from Google Drive into The Drive AI?" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left">
    Not in a single change: the agent can't move or copy files between two different storages. A [workflow](/workflows/overview) with a **Save a Copy** step can copy files across, as they arrive or on a schedule over an existing folder. For a one-off copy, use **New** > **Import from Cloud**, or **Export to Cloud** on a file.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can the agent browse the web or read my email?" icon="globe">
    No. It works on your files in The Drive AI and in connected drives. To file email attachments, use a [workflow](/workflows/overview).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Organizing files

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do my uploads get filed automatically?" icon="folder-tree">
    Only if a workflow does it. Automatic filing on upload was replaced by [workflows](/workflows/overview): an owner or admin sets up a workflow with a **File Uploaded** trigger, and it files each new upload the way you describe.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I tidy up an existing folder?" icon="broom">
    Ask the agent, for example "Organize this folder by client and year." It shows you the changes before or as it makes them. You can also right-click the file browser and choose **Organize this folder (⌘K)**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does Command+K do?" icon="keyboard">
    `⌘K` (or `Ctrl+K`) runs **Organize this folder** on the folder you're looking at. To open the agent, press `⌘J` (or `Ctrl+J`).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Integrations

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What integrations are available?" icon="plug">
    - **Cloud storage:** [Google Drive](/integrations/google-drive), [OneDrive](/integrations/onedrive), SharePoint and [Dropbox](/integrations/dropbox). The agent and workflows work in them directly, and you can import and export files.
    - **Email and messaging:** [Gmail and Outlook](/integrations/email-integration), [Slack](/integrations/slack) and [Microsoft Teams](/integrations/microsoft-teams). Workflows can act on the files that arrive through them.
    - **AI tools:** the [MCP server](/integrations/mcp-server) connects Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and other tools to your files.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="If I connect Gmail, are my attachments saved automatically?" icon="envelope">
    Only when a workflow saves them. Connecting an account lets workflows see what arrives. The workflow decides what's saved and where.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I connect more than one account?" icon="envelopes-bulk">
    Yes. Each integration has **Add account** (or **Add workspace** for Slack).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Workspaces and plans

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is a workspace?" icon="building">
    A workspace is where your files live. You get a personal workspace when you sign up, and only you use it. Team workspaces are for working with other people. See [Members and roles](/account/members-and-roles).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I create a team workspace?" icon="users-gear">
    Click your workspace name at the top of the left sidebar, click **Create team workspace**, and follow the steps.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How much does it cost?" icon="money-bill">
    There's a free plan, paid personal plans, and team plans billed per seat. See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-pricing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who manages billing?" icon="credit-card">
    The workspace owner. Only the owner sees **Settings** > **Billing**. Members don't see prices.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I delete my personal workspace?" icon="trash">
    No. Your personal workspace is tied to your account. The owner of a team workspace can delete it from **Settings** > **Danger Zone**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Account

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I sign in?" icon="key">
    With Google, Microsoft or Apple, or with an email and password. Your account is created the first time you sign in.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I delete my account?" icon="user-xmark">
    Open **Settings** > **Profile**, click **Delete** next to **Delete account**, then **Yes, delete my account**. This deletes your account and its data and removes you from every workspace. If you own a team workspace, transfer ownership or delete that workspace first.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
