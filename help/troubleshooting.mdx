## The agent says it can't see my Google Drive

This applies to OneDrive, SharePoint and Dropbox too. Check these in order:

1. **Your plan includes connected drives.** Connected drives come with Max, Team, Business and Enterprise. On other plans, connecting is blocked and the agent only sees files stored in The Drive AI. See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-pricing).
2. **The drive is connected to this workspace.** Open **Settings** > **Integrations** and look under **Cloud storage**. If the drive says **Not connected**, click it and connect it. Connections belong to a workspace, so if you have several workspaces, check you're in the right one.
3. **It's the right account.** The agent sees the account that was connected. If the file is in a different Google or Microsoft account, click **Add account** and connect that one too.
4. **Cataloguing has finished.** After connecting, the agent builds a catalog of the drive first. In **Settings** > **Integrations** the account shows **Agent: cataloguing** until it's done, then **Agent: ready**. Large drives take longer. If it shows **Agent: needs attention**, open **Settings** > **Agent**, expand **What the agent can see** and click **Re-scan**.
5. **For a Shared Drive or SharePoint library, it's been added and your account can open it.** Your own drive appears automatically. A Google Shared Drive or SharePoint library has to be added by a workspace owner or admin. After that, you reach it through your own account: connect the Google or Microsoft account that's a member of the drive, and it appears the next time you open the agent. If your account isn't a member of the drive, ask its owner in Google Drive or SharePoint for access. See [Connected drives](/agent/connected-drives#personal-drives-and-shared-drives).
6. **It's a file the agent can read.** On connected drives, the agent reads text, PDFs (including scanned ones), Word, Excel and PowerPoint files, and Google Docs, Sheets and Slides. It reads audio and video on plans that include every file type. It can't read the content of images stored there.

In the [browser extension](/agent/browser-extension), a note above the chat tells you which of these applies to the page you're on.

## The agent stopped, or I reached my limit

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="&quot;You've used your assistant tasks for this month&quot;" icon="gauge">
    Every plan includes a number of assistant tasks. A task is a request the assistant acts on; answering its questions or saying hello doesn't count. Every plan includes a monthly number (20 on Free, 500 on Premium, 2,000 on Max, 2,000 per seat on Team and 4,000 per seat on Business, shared across the workspace), and the message tells you the date they come back. You can upgrade for more at any time; in a team workspace, only the owner can. See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-pricing), and check your usage in **Settings** > **Usage** under **Assistant tasks this month**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The agent says a task covers too many files" icon="files">
    Each plan sets how many files the agent can read in one task (50 on the Free plan). Narrow the request to one folder or a smaller set of files, or upgrade.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The agent stopped partway through a big job" icon="circle-pause">
    A single task has a size limit. When the agent reaches it, it stops and tells you where it got to. Ask it to continue, which starts a new task. Owners and admins can choose a bigger **Task size** in **Settings** > **Agent** under **Limits and approvals**. Which sizes you can choose depends on your plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I clicked Stop" icon="stop">
    The conversation shows **Stopped.** Changes already applied stay applied, and you can undo them from their cards. Send a new message to carry on.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="&quot;The agent is not enabled for this workspace&quot;" icon="toggle-off">
    The agent is switched off for this workspace. A workspace owner or admin can turn it on in **Settings** > **Agent** with **Available in this workspace**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A change is waiting and nothing happens" icon="hourglass">
    Look for a card that says **Needs your OK** in the conversation. Click **Apply** (or **Allow**) to go ahead, or **Not now** to skip it. A proposal that isn't applied before the conversation's run ends expires. Ask again if you still want it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Uploads

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Files won't upload" icon="upload">
    - Check your internet connection and try a smaller batch.
    - Check you have storage left in **Settings** > **Usage**. Files in the Trash still count until you delete them there.
    - Your plan limits how many files you can upload at once. The app tells you if you pick too many.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The agent can't read a file I uploaded" icon="file-circle-question">
    - Wait a few minutes. Large files take longer to process.
    - Reading images, audio and video needs a plan that includes every file type, with an active subscription. On a paused or ended team subscription, new uploads of these files are not read until it is reactivated.
    - Password-protected files can't be read. Remove the password and upload again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Connections and workflows

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Connecting an account does nothing" icon="plug-circle-xmark">
    - Allow pop-ups for The Drive AI. Sign-in opens in a new window.
    - Make sure your plan includes connected accounts.
    - For SharePoint, OneDrive for work or Teams, your Microsoft 365 administrator may need to approve The Drive AI first. See [Connected drives](/agent/connected-drives).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Email attachments aren't being saved" icon="envelope">
    Connecting Gmail or Outlook doesn't save attachments by itself. A workflow with an **Email Attachment** trigger has to save them. Check that one exists and is **Active**, and look at its **Activity**. Also check the sender isn't in **Blocked senders** or the workspace **Email Blocklist**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A workflow didn't run on a file" icon="diagram-project">
    - Open the Workflows page and check the workflow is **Active**.
    - Look under **Activity** for the file. Filter by **Needs attention**.
    - Check **Approvals**. A run waiting for a decision stays paused.
    - If the page says **Monthly file limit reached**, the workspace has used its workflow files for the month.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="MCP connection failing" icon="plug">
    - Use the server URL `https://thedriveai.fastmcp.app/mcp`.
    - Sign in with a The Drive AI account that uses Google. Accounts created with Microsoft, Apple or email can't connect yet.
    - Disconnect and reconnect the tool, and sign in to The Drive AI when prompted.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Account and billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can't sign in" icon="right-to-bracket">
    - Allow pop-ups for The Drive AI.
    - Try a private window or another browser.
    - Make sure you use the same sign-in method as before: Google, Microsoft, Apple, or email and password.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't see Billing" icon="credit-card">
    Only the workspace owner sees **Settings** > **Billing**. Ask the owner to change the plan or seats.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My new plan isn't showing" icon="rotate">
    Refresh the page, or sign out and back in. If it still hasn't changed after a few minutes, email [contact@thedrive.ai](mailto:contact@thedrive.ai).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
