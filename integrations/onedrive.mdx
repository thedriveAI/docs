## Overview

Connecting OneDrive lets The Drive AI work with your files where they already are:

- **The agent** can find, read and organize files in OneDrive. Every change is shown on a card in the chat, and you can undo it there. See [Connected drives](/agent/connected-drives#undo-on-each-drive) for what can be undone in OneDrive.
- **Workflows** can start when a file is added to OneDrive, and can save files there. See [Workflows](/workflows/overview).
- **Import and export** copy files between OneDrive and The Drive AI when you want a copy on the other side.

SharePoint document libraries work the same way, through a separate SharePoint connection. See [SharePoint](#sharepoint).

Connected drives and accounts come with Max, Team, Business and Enterprise. See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-pricing).

## Who can do what

| Task | Who |
|------|-----|
| Connect a Microsoft account | Any member who can open **Settings**. Viewers can't. |
| See which accounts are connected, and who connected them | Every member |
| Browse an account, import from it or export to it | Only the person who connected it |
| Add a SharePoint library for the agent | Owners and admins, with a SharePoint account they connected themselves |
| Disconnect an account | The person who connected it, or an owner or admin |

## Before you start: work and school accounts

You can connect a personal Microsoft account or a work or school account.

With a work or school account, your Microsoft 365 administrator has to approve The Drive AI for your organization first. They only do this once. Personal Microsoft accounts don't need this. See [Administrator approval](#administrator-approval).

## Connect OneDrive

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Open **Settings** and choose **Integrations**. Under **Cloud storage**, click **OneDrive**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Click **Connect OneDrive** and sign in with your Microsoft account in the window that opens. If nothing opens, allow pop-ups for The Drive AI and try again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let the agent catalog it">
    When you're done, you see **OneDrive connected** and the account appears under **Connected accounts**. While the agent builds its catalog of your OneDrive, the account shows **Agent: cataloguing**. When it's done, it shows **Agent: ready**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* Screenshot: Settings > Integrations > OneDrive with one connected account showing "Connected by you · Connected <date> · Agent: ready" */}

To connect another Microsoft account, click **Add account** at the bottom of the list. Each account is listed separately, with who connected it, when, and how many workflows use it. Accounts you can't act on show **View only**.

If your plan doesn't include connected drives, clicking **OneDrive** opens a message saying which plans include them, instead of the sign-in window.

### What you allow

Microsoft asks you to let The Drive AI:

- Read and write files your account can already open
- Read your basic profile, to show which account is connected
- Stay connected without asking you to sign in again

The Drive AI uses the account with your own Microsoft permissions. It can't open anything your account can't. The agent only changes files when you ask, and shows each change on a card you can undo.

## Administrator approval

When a work or school account needs approval, you see **Administrator approval is required**. If no account is connected yet, the OneDrive page then says **Administrator approval is pending**.

1. Ask your Microsoft 365 administrator to approve The Drive AI in Microsoft Entra.
2. Once they have, click **Check approval & finish connecting** and sign in again.

## SharePoint

SharePoint is connected separately from OneDrive, and needs a work or school account. There's no personal SharePoint drive: you work with document libraries on SharePoint sites.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect SharePoint">
    In **Settings** > **Integrations**, under **Cloud storage**, click **SharePoint**, then click **Connect SharePoint** and sign in. Your administrator has to approve The Drive AI for SharePoint first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add libraries for the agent (owners and admins)">
    Open **Settings** > **Agent**, find your SharePoint account under **What the agent can see**, and click **Add library**. Search for the site by name or paste a link to it, choose the library, and click **Add library**. Once a library is there, you can also click **+** beside **SharePoint** under **Connected** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each member reaches a library through their own connected SharePoint account, if that account can open it. See [Connected drives](/agent/connected-drives#personal-drives-and-shared-drives).

SharePoint asks you to let The Drive AI read and write SharePoint sites your account can already open, read your basic profile, and stay connected.

## Import files into The Drive AI

Importing makes a copy in The Drive AI. You don't need to import a file for the agent to work on it: the agent can read and organize files in your connected drive where they are.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the folder">
    In the file browser, open the folder you want the files in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the import">
    Click **New** and choose **Import from Cloud**. You can also right-click an empty part of the folder and choose **Import from Cloud**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the account">
    Under **Connected accounts**, click your OneDrive or SharePoint account. Only accounts you connected yourself are listed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="SharePoint only: choose the site and library">
    Under **SharePoint site**, search for the site or paste a link to it. Then choose the **Document library**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose files and folders">
    Browse to what you want and tick it. You can select up to 100 items per import. A selected folder counts as one item and is imported with everything in it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import">
    Leave **Skip duplicates** on, then click **Import** (the button shows how many items, for example **Import 3 items**).
  </Step>
</Steps>

What to expect:

- **Progress** shows under **Transfers** in the left sidebar while the import runs.
- **Skip duplicates** is on by default. With it on, a file whose content is already somewhere in the workspace is skipped, and a folder with the same name already in the same place is reused.
- **Folders** are imported up to 10 levels deep.
- **Imported files are copies.** Your originals stay where they are, and later changes on either side aren't copied across.

## Export files to OneDrive or SharePoint

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose what to export">
    Click **⋮** on a file or folder and choose **Export to Cloud**. To export several at once, select them and click **Export** in the selection bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the account and folder">
    Choose your account. For SharePoint, choose the site and document library. Then open the destination folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    Click **Export here**. Progress shows under **Transfers**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only the person who connected an account can export to it. Viewers can't export.

## Disconnect

In **Settings** > **Integrations**, open **OneDrive** (or **SharePoint**) and click **Disconnect** next to the account. The confirmation lists the workflows that will pause, or says **No workflows depend on this account.**

When you disconnect:

- Workflows that use the account are paused. If the same account is connected again later, workflows paused only because of the disconnect start again on their own.
- The agent stops seeing the drive or libraries. In **Settings** > **Agent**, they show as **Paused**.
- Files you already imported stay in The Drive AI.
- The Drive AI stops using the account, but the access you allowed is still recorded with Microsoft. To remove it there too, remove The Drive AI's access in your Microsoft account settings, or ask your administrator.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Administrator approval is required" icon="user-shield">
    Your organization hasn't approved The Drive AI yet. Ask your Microsoft 365 administrator to approve it in Microsoft Entra, then click **Check approval & finish connecting**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Connecting fails or nothing happens" icon="plug-circle-xmark">
    - If you see **Popup blocked**, allow pop-ups for The Drive AI, then try again.
    - Make sure you sign in with the right Microsoft account.
    - SharePoint only works with a work or school account.
    - If a message about plans appears instead of the sign-in window, your plan doesn't include connected drives.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't browse or import from an account" icon="lock">
    Only the person who connected an account can browse it. If a teammate's account shows **View only**, connect your own Microsoft account with **Add account**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The agent can't find a file" icon="magnifying-glass">
    - Wait until the account shows **Agent: ready** in **Settings** > **Integrations**.
    - If it shows **Agent: needs attention**, open **Settings** > **Agent** to see why, and click **Re-scan**.
    - For SharePoint, check that an owner or admin added the library, and that your own SharePoint account can open it.
    - See [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
