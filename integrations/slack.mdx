## Overview

Connect Slack, and [workflows](/workflows/overview) can act on files shared in your channels: read them, rename them and save them to the right folder.

Connecting Slack doesn't save anything by itself. Files are saved only when a workflow with a **Slack** trigger saves them. The Slack integration is in beta.

<Note>
  Slack needs a plan that includes connected accounts. See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-pricing).
</Note>

## Connect Slack

Only a workspace owner or admin can connect Slack.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Open **Settings** and choose **Integrations**. Under **Email and messaging**, click **Slack**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    Click **Connect Slack**, choose your Slack workspace, and allow the app. You may need to be a Slack admin to install apps. To connect another Slack workspace, click **Add workspace**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the app to channels">
    The app only sees channels it has been added to. In each channel you want covered, type:

    ```text
    /invite @The Drive AI
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up a workflow">
    Create a workflow that starts with **Slack** to decide what happens to shared files. See [Workflows](/workflows/overview).
  </Step>
</Steps>

To stop covering a channel, remove the app from that channel in Slack.

## Import past files

To bring in files shared before you connected, open the connection's **…** menu in **Settings** > **Integrations** > **Slack** and choose **Historical sync**.

## Disconnect

Open the connection's **…** menu and choose **Disconnect**. Files already saved stay in your workspace.

## Privacy

The app only sees channels you add it to. You can remove it from your Slack workspace at any time in Slack's settings.
