## Overview

Connect Slack, and [workflows](/workflows/overview) can act on files shared in your channels: read them, rename them and save them to the right folder. Workflows can also post messages to Slack.

Connecting Slack doesn't save anything by itself. Files are saved only when a workflow with a **Slack** trigger saves them, or when you run a [historical import](#import-past-files). The Slack integration is in beta.

Connected drives and accounts come with Max, Team, Business and Enterprise. See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-pricing).

## Who can do what

| Task | Who |
|------|-----|
| Connect a Slack workspace | Workspace owners and admins |
| See which Slack workspaces are connected | Every member who can open **Settings** |
| Run a historical import | The person who connected the Slack workspace |
| Disconnect | The person who connected it, or an owner or admin |

## Connect Slack

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Open **Settings** and choose **Integrations**. Under **Email and messaging**, click **Slack**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    Click **Connect Slack**. In the window that opens, choose your Slack workspace and allow the app. Your Slack workspace's settings may require a Slack admin to approve new apps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the app to channels">
    The app only sees channels it has been added to. In each channel you want covered, type:

    ```text
    /invite @The Drive AI
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up a workflow">
    Create a workflow that starts with **Slack** to decide what happens to shared files. See [Triggers](/workflows/triggers).
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* Screenshot: Settings > Integrations > Slack with one connected Slack workspace and the note about /invite @The Drive AI below it */}

After you connect, the **Historical Sync** panel opens so you can bring in files shared before. You can close it and come back later.

### More than one Slack workspace

You can connect several Slack workspaces to one workspace in The Drive AI, including a personal workspace. Click **Add workspace** at the bottom of the list. Each one is listed by its Slack workspace name.

### What you allow

When you install the app, Slack asks you to let The Drive AI:

- See the public and private channels it has been added to, and read their messages and shared files
- Post messages, including in public channels it hasn't been added to
- See people's names and email addresses in your Slack workspace

The app is installed for the whole Slack workspace, not just for you. If the person who connected it leaves your workspace in The Drive AI, the Slack connection keeps working.

## Choose which channels are covered

A channel is covered once the app is in it. To stop covering a channel, remove the app from that channel in Slack.

When you set up a workflow with a **Slack** trigger, you choose the channels it watches, or **All channels**, meaning every channel the app is a member of.

## Post messages from a workflow

A workflow's **Send a Message** step can post to a Slack channel, for example to tell your team a signed contract arrived. See [Steps](/workflows/steps).

## Import past files

To bring in files shared before you connected:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Historical sync">
    In **Settings** > **Integrations** > **Slack**, open the **…** menu on the Slack workspace and choose **Historical sync**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a time range">
    Choose **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days** or **Last 180 days (6 months)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start">
    Click **Start Import**. It runs in the background, so you can close the panel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

What to expect:

- **Which channels:** every channel the app is a member of, public or private, except archived channels.
- **Where files go:** the top level of your workspace, as they are. Imported files don't start **Slack** workflows. A workflow that watches uploads to The Drive AI can file them.
- **Skipped:** images, calendar files, files already imported, and files found to contain malware.
- **Progress:** reopen **Historical sync** to see progress, or click **Cancel Sync** to stop. Owners and admins can see every imported file in **Settings** > **Import History**.

## Disconnect

Open the Slack workspace's **…** menu and choose **Disconnect**, then confirm. Owners and admins can also click **Disconnect** on a Slack workspace someone else connected.

- Workflows that use it are paused. If the same Slack workspace is connected again later, workflows paused only because of the disconnect start again on their own.
- Files already saved stay in your workspace.
- The app stays installed in Slack. To remove it, remove it from your Slack workspace in Slack.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Files shared in a channel aren't picked up" icon="hashtag">
    - Check that the app is in the channel. Type `/invite @The Drive AI` in it.
    - Check that a workflow with a **Slack** trigger is active and watches that channel. See [Approvals and activity](/workflows/approvals-and-activity) for its runs.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't connect Slack" icon="plug-circle-xmark">
    - Only workspace owners and admins can connect Slack.
    - If you see **Popup blocked**, allow pop-ups for The Drive AI.
    - Your Slack workspace may need a Slack admin to approve the app.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A historical import found nothing" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    The import covers just the channels the app is in. Add the app to the channels first, then start the import again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
