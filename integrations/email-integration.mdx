## Overview

Connect a Gmail or Outlook account, and [workflows](/workflows/overview) can act on the attachments that arrive in it. A workflow can read an attachment, decide what it is, rename it and save it to the right folder.

Connecting an account doesn't save anything by itself. Attachments are saved only when a workflow with an **Email Attachment** trigger saves them.

<Note>
  Email integration needs a plan that includes connected accounts. See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-pricing).
</Note>

## Connect Gmail or Outlook

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Open **Settings** and choose **Integrations**. Under **Email and messaging**, click **Gmail** or **Outlook**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Click **Connect Gmail** (or **Connect Outlook**) and sign in to your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up a workflow">
    Create a workflow that starts with **Email Attachment** to decide what happens to incoming attachments. Only an owner or admin can create workflows. See [Workflows](/workflows/overview).
  </Step>
</Steps>

In a team workspace, click **Add account** to connect more accounts. A personal workspace can connect one Gmail account and one Outlook account. Every member can see which email accounts are connected to the workspace.

In team workspaces, an owner or admin can switch on **Require approval for new connections**. New email connections then wait until an admin clicks **Approve**.

## Import past attachments

Workflows handle mail that arrives after you connect. To bring in attachments you already received:

1. In **Settings** > **Integrations**, open **Gmail** or **Outlook**.
2. Open the **…** menu on your account and choose **Historical sync**.
3. Choose a **Time range**, then click **Start Import**.

Imported attachments are saved to the top level of your workspace. Owners and admins can review past imports in **Settings** > **Import History**.

## Block senders

- **For one account:** open the account's **…** menu and choose **Blocked senders**, then add email addresses or whole domains.
- **For the whole workspace:** owners and admins can open **Settings** > **Email Blocklist**.

## Disconnect

Open the account's **…** menu and choose **Disconnect**. You can disconnect accounts you connected. Owners and admins can disconnect any account. Attachments already saved stay in your workspace.

## Privacy

The Drive AI connects through Google or Microsoft sign-in and never sees your password. You can also revoke access at any time from your Google or Microsoft account settings.
