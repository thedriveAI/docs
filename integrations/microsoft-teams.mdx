## Overview

Connect Microsoft Teams, and [workflows](/workflows/overview) can act on files shared in your Teams channels: read them, rename them and save them to the right folder.

Connecting Teams doesn't save anything by itself. Files are saved only when a workflow with a **Microsoft Teams** trigger saves them. The Teams integration is in beta.

<Note>
  Microsoft Teams needs a plan that includes connected accounts. See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-pricing).
</Note>

## Connect Microsoft Teams

Only a workspace owner or admin can connect Teams.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Open **Settings** and choose **Integrations**. Under **Email and messaging**, click **Teams**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Click **Connect Teams** and sign in with your Microsoft account. To connect another account, click **Add Microsoft account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose which teams are covered">
    Owners and admins can choose which teams the connection covers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up a workflow">
    Create a workflow that starts with **Microsoft Teams** to decide what happens to shared files. See [Workflows](/workflows/overview).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Some Microsoft 365 organizations need an administrator to approve The Drive AI before anyone can connect. If sign-in asks for admin approval, ask your Microsoft 365 administrator.
</Note>

## Import past files

To bring in files shared before you connected, open the connection's **…** menu in **Settings** > **Integrations** > **Teams** and choose **Historical sync**.

## Disconnect

Open the connection's **…** menu and choose **Disconnect**. Files already saved stay in your workspace.
