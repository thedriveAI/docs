## Overview

Connect Microsoft Teams, and [workflows](/workflows/overview) can act on files shared in your Teams channels: read them, rename them and save them to the right folder.

Connecting Teams doesn't save anything by itself. Files are saved only when a workflow with a **Microsoft Teams** trigger saves them, or when you run a [historical import](#import-past-files). The Teams integration is in beta.

Connected drives and accounts come with Max, Team, Business and Enterprise. See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-pricing).

## Before you start

- **One connection per workspace.** A workspace in The Drive AI can have one Teams connection, made with one Microsoft account. It covers teams that account belongs to.
- **A work or school account with Teams.** Personal Microsoft accounts, such as Outlook.com, Hotmail.com and Live.com, can't be connected. The account needs a Microsoft Teams license.
- **Administrator approval.** Your Microsoft 365 administrator always has to approve The Drive AI for your organization before Teams can be connected. Ask them before you start.

## Who can do what

| Task | Who |
|------|-----|
| Connect Teams | Workspace owners and admins |
| See the connection and which teams it covers | Every member who can open **Settings** |
| Change which teams are covered | Owners and admins, in team workspaces |
| Run a historical import | The person who connected Teams |
| Disconnect | The person who connected it, or an owner or admin |

## Connect Microsoft Teams

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Open **Settings** and choose **Integrations**. Under **Email and messaging**, click **Teams**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Click **Connect Teams** and sign in with your work or school Microsoft account in the window that opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check which teams are covered">
    To start with, the connection covers every team the account belongs to when you connect. The connection is listed by the teams it covers, with the Microsoft account under it. In a team workspace, narrow it down if you need to. See [Choose which teams are covered](#choose-which-teams-are-covered).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up a workflow">
    Create a workflow that starts with **Microsoft Teams**, and choose the team and channels it watches. See [Triggers](/workflows/triggers).
  </Step>
</Steps>

  <img src="/images/integrations-teams.png" alt="Settings, Integrations, Teams, with the connection and the teams it covers" />
</Frame>

Connecting doesn't start watching any channel. A channel is only watched once a workflow asks for it.

### What you allow

Microsoft asks you to let The Drive AI:

- Read messages in the channels being watched
- Read files your account can already open
- List the teams you belong to, and the channels in them
- Read your basic profile
- Stay connected without asking you to sign in again

## Choose which teams are covered

In a team workspace, owners and admins can limit which teams the connection covers:

1. In **Settings** > **Integrations** > **Teams**, click the button under the connection that names the covered teams.
2. Tick or untick teams. The list stays open so you can change several.

What to know:

- **Workflows can only watch channels in these teams.** Nothing is watched until a workflow asks for it.
- **The last team can't be unticked.** A connection has to cover at least one team. To stop it entirely, disconnect the account.
- **Unticking a team pauses workflows that watch it.** You see which ones. Tick the team again and they become active again.
- **A team the account has left** stays in the list marked **no longer available**, so you can untick it.

A team the account joins later isn't added on its own. In a team workspace, an owner or admin ticks it. In a personal workspace, the connection shows which teams it covers, but the list can't be changed.

## Import past files

To bring in files shared before you connected:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Historical sync">
    In **Settings** > **Integrations** > **Teams**, open the **…** menu on the connection and choose **Historical sync**. The panel says which teams it covers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a time range">
    Choose **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days** or **Last 180 days (6 months)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start">
    Click **Start Import**. It runs in the background, so you can close the panel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

What to expect:

- **Which channels:** the channels of the teams the connection covers. If no teams are covered, the panel says nothing would be imported.
- **Where files go:** the top level of your workspace, as they are. Imported files don't start **Microsoft Teams** workflows. A workflow that watches uploads to The Drive AI can file them.
- **Skipped:** images, calendar files, files already imported, and files found to contain malware.
- **Progress:** reopen **Historical sync** to see progress, or click **Cancel Sync** to stop. Owners and admins can see every imported file in **Settings** > **Import History**.

## Disconnect

Open the connection's **…** menu and choose **Disconnect**, then confirm. Owners and admins can also click **Disconnect** on a connection someone else made.

- Workflows that use it are paused. If the same account is connected again later, workflows paused only because of the disconnect start again on their own.
- Files already saved stay in your workspace.
- Once disconnected, an owner or admin can connect Teams again, with the same or a different account.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Teams wasn't connected" icon="plug-circle-xmark">
    The window that opens tells you why:

    - **Teams access requires administrator approval** or **Teams access was not approved:** ask your Microsoft 365 administrator to approve The Drive AI, then try again.
    - **Microsoft Teams requires a work or school account:** sign in with your work or school account instead of a personal one.
    - **We couldn't connect Microsoft Teams:** check whether Teams is already connected to this workspace. Only one connection is allowed. Otherwise, try again or contact your workspace administrator.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Files shared in a channel aren't picked up" icon="hashtag">
    - Check that a workflow with a **Microsoft Teams** trigger is active and watches that channel.
    - Check that the channel's team is covered by the connection.
    - If the connection shows that live updates are paused, The Drive AI is reconnecting by itself.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A workflow was paused after I changed the teams" icon="pause">
    It watches a team the connection no longer covers. Tick that team again, or change the workflow to watch a covered team.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
