## Overview

Connecting Dropbox lets The Drive AI work with your files where they already are:

- **The agent** can find, read and organize files in Dropbox. Every change is shown on a card in the chat, and you can undo it there. See [Connected drives](/agent/connected-drives#undo-on-each-drive) for what can be undone in Dropbox.
- **Workflows** can start when a file is added to Dropbox, and can save files there. See [Workflows](/workflows/overview).
- **Import and export** copy files between Dropbox and The Drive AI when you want a copy on the other side.

Connecting Dropbox needs a plan that includes connected drives. See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-pricing).

## Connect Dropbox

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Open **Settings** and choose **Integrations**. Under **Cloud storage**, click **Dropbox**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Click **Connect Dropbox** and sign in with your Dropbox account in the window that opens. If nothing opens, allow pop-ups for The Drive AI and try again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let the agent catalog it">
    The account appears in the list. While the agent builds its catalog, the account shows **Agent: cataloguing**. When it's done, it shows **Agent: ready**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To connect another Dropbox account, click **Add account**. Every member can see which accounts are connected to the workspace, and who connected them. Only the person who connected an account can browse it or import from it.

## Import files into The Drive AI

1. In the file browser, open the folder you want the files in.
2. Click **New** and choose **Import from Cloud**.
3. Pick the Dropbox account, browse to the files or folders, and select them.
4. Leave **Skip duplicates** on so files you've already imported are skipped, then click **Import**.

Imported files are copies. Your originals in Dropbox stay where they are, and later changes on either side aren't copied across. You can follow progress under **Transfers** in the sidebar.

## Export files to Dropbox

1. Click **⋮** on a file and choose **Export to Cloud**, or select several files and click **Export**.
2. Choose the Dropbox account and the destination folder, then click **Export here**.

## Disconnect

In **Settings** > **Integrations**, open **Dropbox** and click **Disconnect** next to the account.

- You can disconnect accounts you connected. Owners and admins can disconnect any account.
- Workflows that use the account are paused, and the confirmation lists them.
- Files you already imported stay in The Drive AI.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Connecting fails or nothing happens" icon="plug-circle-xmark">
    - Allow pop-ups for The Drive AI, then try again.
    - Make sure you sign in with the right Dropbox account.
    - Check that your plan includes connected drives.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The agent can't find a file" icon="magnifying-glass">
    - Wait until the account shows **Agent: ready** in **Settings** > **Integrations**.
    - See [The agent says it can't see my drive](/help/troubleshooting#the-agent-says-it-cant-see-my-google-drive).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
