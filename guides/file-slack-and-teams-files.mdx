---
title: "File Slack and Microsoft Teams Files Automatically with The Drive AI"
description: "A workflow in The Drive AI saves each file shared in a Slack or Teams channel, reads it, and renames and files it into the right folder"
---

The Drive AI files the documents people share in Slack and Microsoft Teams channels. A workflow saves each shared file as it's posted, reads it to work out what it is, then renames it and files it into a folder in The Drive AI or a connected Google Drive, OneDrive, SharePoint or Dropbox. It can also post a message back to Slack. Slack and Teams connections come with Max, Team, Business and Enterprise, and both are in beta.

## What you need

- **A plan with connected accounts:** Max, Team, Business or Enterprise. See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-pricing).
- **An owner or admin** to connect Slack or Teams and to create the workflow.
- **For Slack:** the app installed in your Slack workspace and added to each channel with `/invite @The Drive AI`. Your Slack admin may need to approve the app.
- **For Teams:** a work or school Microsoft account with a Teams license, and your Microsoft 365 administrator's approval. Personal Microsoft accounts can't be connected.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Slack or Teams">
    Open **Settings** > **Integrations** and click **Slack** or **Teams**. See [Slack](/integrations/slack) or [Microsoft Teams](/integrations/microsoft-teams). Connecting saves nothing by itself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the workflow">
    Open **Workflows** and describe it, or ask the agent, for example "When a file is shared in #contracts, save it, check whether it's a signed contract, then file it under Clients by client name and year." See [Create a workflow](/workflows/overview#create-a-workflow).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the steps">
    The trigger is **Slack** or **Microsoft Teams**, set to the channels to watch. The first step has to be **Save a Copy**, because a shared file isn't stored anywhere until the workflow saves it. Then **Ask AI About the File**, **Rename** and **Move** file it. See [Triggers](/workflows/triggers#slack).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Save and switch it on. Each file's run appears in the workflow's **Activity**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Files shared before you connected

**Historical sync** in **Settings** > **Integrations** brings in files from the last 30, 90 or 180 days. They land at the top level of the workspace as they are, and don't start Slack or Teams workflows. A workflow that watches uploads to The Drive AI can file them.

## What it can't do

- **It only sees channels it's in.** In Slack, a channel is covered once the app is added. In Teams, only teams the connection covers can be watched.
- **Historical sync skips images** and files already imported, and doesn't file what it brings in.
- **Each file a workflow handles counts as a workflow file.** See [Workflow files](/workflows/workflow-files).
- **AI reading can occasionally be wrong.** Add **Ask a Person to Approve** before **Move** for anything that matters.
