## Overview

A file request collects documents from clients, vendors or anyone else. Each recipient gets an email with their own upload link. They open the link, upload their files, and the files land in the folder you chose. Recipients don't need an account.

You can ask for specific documents with a checklist, set a due date, rename uploads automatically, and send reminders to people who haven't finished.

<Note>
  Creating file requests needs a plan that includes them. See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-pricing).
</Note>

## Who can create and manage requests

| Action | Who can do it |
|--------|---------------|
| Create a request | Owners, admins and members. Viewers and guests can't. |
| See requests | Owners, admins and members see every request in the workspace, including other people's. |
| Close a request, add or remove recipients, send reminders | The person who created the request, and workspace owners and admins |

If you open someone else's request and you're a member, you can see its progress, but closing it, reminding or changing its recipients is refused.

## Where to find file requests

Open **Settings** and choose **File Requests**. Each request shows its title, status, how many recipients have responded, how many files have arrived, and when it was created.

## Create a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new request">
    In **Settings** > **File Requests**, click **New request**. The **Request files** form opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    | Field | What it does | Default |
    |-------|--------------|---------|
    | **Title** | Required. What you're asking for, for example "Q4 Tax Documents". Recipients see it in the email and on the upload page. | Empty |
    | **Message to recipients** | Optional instructions shown in the email and on the upload page | Empty |
    | **Recipients** | An email (required) and a name (optional) for each person. Click **Add recipient** for more. | One empty row |
    | **Required documents** | Optional checklist of documents to collect. See [Checklists](#checklists). | No checklist |
    | **Destination** | The folder uploads go to. Search for a folder, or choose **Workspace root**. | **Root** |
    | **Expires** | Optional date after which the upload links stop working | **No expiry** |

    If the same email appears twice, it's only added once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set more options (optional)">
    Click **More options**:

    | Option | What it does | Default |
    |--------|--------------|---------|
    | **Rename uploaded files** | Renames each upload with a pattern. See [Rename uploads](#rename-uploads). | Off (files keep their names) |
    | **Smart reminders** | Emails recipients who still owe files: **Every day**, **Every 2 days**, **Every 3 days**, **Every 5 days** or **Weekly** | Off. When you turn it on, **Every 3 days**. |
    | **Completion email** | Emails you when a recipient has uploaded every required document on the checklist | On |
    | **Save as template** | Saves the checklist so you can reuse it. Shown when the request has a checklist. Enter a template name. | Off |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Send Request**. Each recipient gets an email with their own link. The **File Request Created** screen lists every recipient with **Copy link**, so you can also share the links yourself.

    Each link is personal. Uploads through it are recorded against that recipient.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Checklists

A checklist lists the documents you need, such as "W-2 Form" or "Photo ID". Each item becomes its own upload slot on the recipient's page, and you can track what's arrived item by item.

- Click **Add document** for each item and type its name.
- Click the speech-bubble icon (**Add helper text**) to add a short hint under an item.
- Click **X** to remove an item.

Items you add in the form are required. Recipients can still upload extra files outside the checklist.

### Templates

A template saves a checklist (item names and helper text) for reuse.

- **Save one:** turn on **Save as template** under **More options** and give it a name. It's saved when you click **Send Request**.
- **Use one:** in **Required documents**, choose **Use template** and pick a template. It fills in the checklist, which you can then edit. **Use template** appears when the checklist is empty and the workspace has saved templates.

Templates are shared with everyone in the workspace who can create requests.

### Rename uploads

Build a pattern from text and these variables. The file's extension is always kept.

| Variable | Button | Becomes |
|----------|--------|---------|
| `{original_name}` | **Original name** | The file's name, without its extension |
| `{recipient_name}` | **Recipient** | The recipient's name, or the part of their email before the @ |
| `{recipient_email}` | **Email** | The recipient's email |
| `{date}` | **Date** | The upload date, as YYYY-MM-DD |
| `{request_title}` | **Title** | The request's title |
| `{slot_name}` | **Document name** | The checklist item's name. For uploads outside the checklist, the file's name. |

For example, `{recipient_name}_{slot_name}` turns a W-2 uploaded by Jane into `Jane_W-2 Form.pdf`.

## What recipients see

<Steps>
  <Step title="The email">
    The subject is "*Your name* is requesting files from you: *title*". It includes your message, the checklist and the due date, if you set them, and an **Upload Files** button that opens their upload page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The upload page">
    The page shows who's asking, the title, your message and the due date.

    - **With a checklist:** each item has an **Upload** button. They pick one file per item, then click **Submit N documents**. A filled item shows **Replace**, to swap the file. Once every required item is in, the checklist shows **Complete**. To send files that aren't on the list, they open **Upload additional files**.
    - **Without a checklist:** they drop files on the page or click **browse**, then click **Upload N files**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="After uploading">
    They see **Files uploaded** and can click **Upload more**. They can come back to the same link until it expires or you close the request.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Upload limits

| Limit | Value |
|-------|-------|
| File size | 500 MB per file |
| Files per upload | 100 |
| Uploads per link | 20 per hour. Each checklist item submitted counts as one upload. |
| File types | Any |

Uploaded files count toward your workspace's storage. If the workspace is full, the recipient sees "The workspace has reached its storage limit. Please contact the person who requested these files." Free up space or upgrade, then ask them to try again. See [Usage and Limits](/account/usage-and-limits).

Files that fail the malware scan are discarded and don't appear in your workspace.

## Where uploads go

Files go into the **Destination** folder you chose. They're named by your rename pattern, if you set one.

When a recipient replaces a checklist item, the new file takes that item's place on the checklist. The earlier file stays in the folder.

## Notifications

| When | In-app notification | Email |
|------|---------------------|-------|
| A recipient uploads a file | Yes, one per file, to the person who created the request | No |
| A recipient uploads every required checklist item | Yes, to the person who created the request | Yes, if **Completion email** is on. The subject is "All documents received from *recipient* for *title*". |

Requests without a checklist don't send a completion notice. Check the request's progress instead.

## Track a request

Click a request in **Settings** > **File Requests** to open it. You see its status, your message, and a summary: files received, how many recipients are complete (with a checklist), the due date and the destination folder.

### Request statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Active** | Links work and recipients can upload |
| **Closed** | You closed it. Links no longer work. |
| **Expired** | The due date passed. Links no longer work. |

### Recipient progress

| Request type | Labels |
|--------------|--------|
| With a checklist | **Pending** (nothing yet), **In progress** (some required items in), **Complete** (every required item in) |
| Without a checklist | **Pending**, **Received** |

Each recipient also shows how many files they've sent and when they last uploaded. Click the arrow next to a recipient to see their files. With a checklist, you see each item with the file received, or **Missing** or **Optional**.

## Manage a request

These actions are available while a request is **Active**:

- **Copy upload link**: copy one recipient's personal link.
- **Send reminder**: email one recipient who still owes files.
- **Remind all (N)**: email every recipient who still owes files. N is how many that is.
- **Add recipient**: enter an email and an optional name, then click **Add**. They get the request email with their own link.
- **Remove recipient**: their link stops working right away. Files they've already uploaded stay in your workspace.
- **Close request**: confirm in **Close this file request?**. All upload links stop working and no more reminders go out. Files already received stay. Closing can't be undone.

Who "still owes files": with a checklist, anyone missing a required item; without one, anyone who hasn't uploaded anything.

You can't change a request's title, message, checklist, folder or due date after sending. To change them, close it and send a new one.

### Reminders

A reminder email with a checklist lists the items and which ones are still missing. Without a checklist, the recipient gets the original request email again.

**Smart reminders** run once a day. They go to recipients who still owe files and haven't been reminded within the interval you chose. The first one can go out the next time they run. They stop when the recipient is done, or when the request closes or expires.

## File requests through the agent

You can ask the [agent](/agent/overview) to create and follow requests, for example: "Ask jane@example.com and sam@example.com for their 2025 W-2 and 1099 by October 31, into /Clients/Tax, and remind them every 3 days."

The agent can:

- **Create a request** with a title, message, recipients, destination folder, due date, reminder interval and checklist. It can mark checklist items optional, which the form can't.
- **List requests** and **check one**: who has sent what, and when they were last reminded.
- **Add recipients**, **send reminders** and **close a request**, if you created it or you're an admin.

Every action that emails someone or closes a request shows you a card first. Click **Allow** to go ahead, or **Not now**. The upload links appear on the card for you; the agent doesn't see them.

<Note>
  Uploads can only go to folders in your workspace, not to folders in a connected drive.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A recipient says their link doesn't work">
    The page tells them why:

    - **Request expired**: the due date passed.
    - **Request closed**: the request was closed.
    - **Invalid upload link**: you removed them from the request, or the link was copied incorrectly.

    To collect more files, send a new request.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title='A recipient sees "Too many uploads"'>
    Each link allows 20 uploads an hour. Ask them to wait and try again, or to upload several files at once instead of one at a time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title='A recipient sees "Upload limit exceeded"'>
    A file is over 500 MB. Ask them to compress or split it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I didn't get a completion email">
    Completion emails are only sent for requests with a checklist, when a recipient has uploaded every required item, and only if **Completion email** was on.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't close, remind or change recipients on a request">
    Only the person who created the request, and workspace owners and admins, can do that. Requests that are **Closed** or **Expired** can't be changed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
