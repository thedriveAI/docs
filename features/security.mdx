## Overview

The Drive AI is built with security at every layer. Your files are protected by encryption, malware scanning, and strict privacy controls. Your data is never used to train AI models.

## Encryption

### Data at Rest

All files stored in The Drive AI are encrypted using **AES-256** encryption—the same standard used by banks and government agencies.

### Data in Transit

All data sent between your device and The Drive AI is encrypted with **TLS**.

| Layer | Standard | Details |
|-------|----------|---------|
| **At rest** | AES-256 | Files encrypted on storage servers |
| **In transit** | TLS | Connections to The Drive AI are encrypted |

## Threat Detection

### Malware Scanning

Files uploaded to The Drive AI, including email attachments that workflows save and files sent through file requests, are scanned for malware. A file found to be infected is rejected or removed.

Files in connected drives stay with Google, Microsoft or Dropbox and aren't scanned by The Drive AI.

## Your Data and AI

<Info>
  **Your data is never used to train AI models.** The Drive AI processes your files to provide features like search, organization, and chat—but your content is never used as training data for any AI model.
</Info>

Your files are:

- **Processed only for your benefit** — AI reads your files to organize, search, and answer questions
- **Never sold** — your content is never sold or used for advertising
- **Never used for model training** — no AI model is trained on your data
- **Isolated per workspace** — team workspaces are fully isolated from each other

## Access Controls

### Authentication

The Drive AI supports secure authentication through:

- Google sign-in
- Microsoft sign-in
- Apple sign-in
- Email and password

### Workspace Isolation

Each workspace is fully isolated:

- Personal workspace files are private to you
- Team workspace files are only accessible to workspace members
- External collaborators can only see explicitly shared items, and anyone with a [public link](/features/file-sharing#public-links) can view that published file
- No cross-workspace data access

### Role-Based Access

Team workspaces have roles (Owner, Admin, Member and Viewer) that set what each person can do. See [Members and roles](/account/members-and-roles).

### File-Level Permissions

Granular permission controls on individual files and folders:

- Share with **Read**, **Write** or **Full Access** per file or folder
- Change or remove access at any time
- See who has access from the **Share** dialog

## Infrastructure

The Drive AI runs on enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure with:

- Redundant storage across multiple data centers
- Automatic backups
- High availability architecture
- Regular security audits

## Privacy Practices

- **Minimal data collection** — we only collect what is needed to provide the service
- **No advertising** — your data is never used for ads or sold to third parties
- **Transparent policies** — clear privacy policy and terms of service
- **Data deletion** — delete your account and its data at any time from **Settings** > **Profile**

## Reporting Security Issues

If you discover a security vulnerability, please contact us at [contact@thedrive.ai](mailto:contact@thedrive.ai). We take all security reports seriously and will respond promptly.
