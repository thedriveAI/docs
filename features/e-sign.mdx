## Overview

Send any PDF in your workspace for electronic signatures. Recipients sign in their browser, with no account or software needed.

<Note>
  Sending documents for signature needs a plan that includes e-sign. See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-pricing). Viewers and guests can't send signature requests.
</Note>

## Start a signature request

- **From the file browser:** click **⋮** on a PDF and choose **Request Signature**.
- **From the PDF viewer:** open the PDF and click the **Request Signature** button in the toolbar.
- **From the agent:** ask, for example, "Send this NDA to jane@example.com for signature." The agent prepares a draft, shows where the fields go, and sends it only after you allow it.

If you have saved templates, you're asked first whether to **Start from scratch** or use a template.

## Prepare and send

<Steps>
  <Step title="Recipients">
    - **Subject**: the email subject, "Please sign: …" by default. Click **Add a message** to include a note.
    - **Recipients**: an email and optional name for each person, as a **Signer** or **CC**
    - **Settings**:
      - Signing order: **Sign in any order** or **Sign in sequence**
      - **Expires**: an optional expiry date
      - **Auto-remind**: **Off**, **Every day**, **Every 2 days**, **Every 3 days** or **Every week**
      - **Require email verification**: signers enter a one-time code sent to their email before they can sign

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/e-sign-3.png" alt="The Recipients step with subject, recipients and settings" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place Fields">
    Click the document to place fields: **Signature**, **Initials**, **Text**, **Date**, **Checkbox** and **Dropdown**. Assign each field to a recipient. You can resize and move fields, and mark them required.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/e-sign-4.png" alt="Placing signature fields on the document" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Send to N recipients**. To finish later, click **Save draft**. To reuse this setup, click **Save as Template**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Track requests

Open **Settings** > **Signatures** to see every request you've sent. Filter by **All**, **Waiting**, **Completed** and **Voided**. Open a request to see each signer's progress and its audit trail.

Saved templates are in **Settings** > **Signing Templates**.

## What recipients do

1. They open the secure link in the email.
2. They review and accept the electronic signature disclosure.
3. They fill in the fields assigned to them and sign.

## Identity Verification

For added security, enable email-based identity verification:

- Recipients receive a one-time 6-digit code to their email
- They must enter the code before accessing the document
- Adds an extra layer of identity assurance

## Legal Compliance

The Drive AI's E-Sign captures a complete audit trail for every document:

| Detail | What Is Recorded |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Consent** | Explicit ESIGN Act consent with timestamp and IP address |
| **Document integrity** | SHA-256 hash at every state transition |
| **Action log** | Every action recorded: viewed, filled, signed, or declined |
| **Audit trail** | Immutable record of all events |

Compliant with:

- **ESIGN Act** (Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act) — legally binding in the United States
- **eIDAS Regulation** — recognized in the European Union

<Info>
  Electronic signatures created through The Drive AI carry the same legal weight as handwritten signatures for most documents.
</Info>

## Post-Signature

After all parties sign:

- The signed PDF is saved as a new file in your workspace (the original remains unchanged)
- All parties receive an email with a link to the completed document
- The full audit trail is attached to the document
