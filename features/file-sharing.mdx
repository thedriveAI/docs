## Overview

There are two ways to let someone outside your workspace see a file or folder:

- **Share it by email** with specific people, choosing whether they can view, edit or manage it.
- **[Publish it](#public-links)** so anyone with the link can view it, without an account.

People in your workspace don't need either. They already reach its files through their [workspace role](/account/members-and-roles), so sharing is for people outside it.

## Who can share

You can share an item, and change who it's shared with, when you have **Full Access** to it or own it:

- **Owners, admins and members** of the workspace have this on its files through their role.
- **People outside the workspace** have it on items shared with them at **Full Access**.
- **Viewers** can't share. They can open the **Share** dialog to see who has access, but can't change it.

## Share a file or folder

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open sharing">
    Click **⋮** on the file or folder and choose **Share**. For the file or folder you have open, you can also click the **Share** button in the top toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter an email">
    Type the person's email under **Invite with email address**. People you've shared with before are suggested as you type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a permission">
    Pick **Full Access**, **Write** or **Read** from the menu next to the email. It starts at **Full Access**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Enter">
    The person is added to the list below and sent an email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/share-dialog.png" alt="The Share dialog with an email typed and the permission menu open showing Full Access, Write and Read" />
</Frame>

<Warning>
  New shares start at **Full Access**, which lets the person share the item with others and publish it. Choose **Read** or **Write** before you press Enter unless you want that.
</Warning>

### Permissions

| Permission | What they can do |
|------------|------------------|
| **Read** | Open and download it |
| **Write** | Also change its content |
| **Full Access** | Also share it with others, change or remove other people's access, and [publish it](#public-links) |
| **Owner** | Shown for the person who owns the item. It can't be changed or removed. |

### Sharing a folder

Sharing a folder shares everything in it, including files and subfolders added later. To stop sharing, remove the person from the folder itself. Their access to everything inside goes too.

Moving an item changes who it's shared with through folders. Moved out of a shared folder, it's no longer shared with that folder's people. Moved into one, it's shared with them. A share you set on the item itself moves with it.

### Who you can't add

- **Workspace members.** You see **Already a workspace member**: they reach the item through their role. In a team workspace the dialog reminds you: **All workspace members already have access to this content based on their role.**
- **Someone already on the list.** You see **File is already shared with** and their email. Change their permission instead.
- **Yourself.**

## What the people you share with get

- **If they have a The Drive AI account**, they get an email saying you've shared the file or folder, with a link to it, and a notification in the app.
- **If they don't**, they get an email inviting them to sign up. Once they sign up with that email address, the item is waiting for them.

Either way, the item appears under **Shared** in their left sidebar. They see only what you shared, not the rest of your workspace.

## Change or remove access

Open the **Share** dialog. Everyone who has access is listed with their permission.

- **To change** someone's permission, pick a new one from the menu next to their name. It takes effect straight away.
- **To remove** someone, click the trash icon (**Delete**) next to their name.

If an item is shared through a folder above it, change or remove the share on that folder.

## Public links

Publishing a file gives it a public link. Anyone with the link can view it, without signing in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the file">
    Open the file you want to publish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Publish">
    Click the **Publish** button (globe icon) in the top toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch it on">
    Switch on **Publish document**. The dialog shows **Published** and the **Public URL**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the link">
    Click the copy button next to the link (**Copy URL**) and send it to whoever needs it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

While a file is published, the **Publish** button shows a green dot. To stop sharing the link, open **Publish** and switch it off. The link stops working.

- **Who can publish:** people with **Full Access** to the file, or its owner. For everyone else the **Publish** button is greyed out.
- **Folders:** the **Publish** button is for files. To publish a folder and everything in it, ask the agent: "Give me a public link to this folder." Files you upload into a published folder later are published too.

## Files on connected drives

Files in Google Drive, OneDrive, SharePoint or Dropbox can't be shared or published from The Drive AI. Share them in the drive itself. See [Connected drives](/agent/connected-drives).

## Sharing through the agent

You can also ask the [agent](/agent/overview):

```text
Share the Q4 report with sam@example.com so they can edit it
```

```text
Who can see the Contracts folder?
```

```text
Stop sharing this folder with alex@example.com
```

```text
Give me a shareable link to this file
```

The agent uses the same rules as the **Share** dialog. Before it shares, removes access or publishes anything, it shows a card with what it will do, who gets access and at what level, and a warning when a share covers a whole folder. Nothing happens until you allow it. A request for "a link" makes a [public link](#public-links); to share with specific people, name their emails.
