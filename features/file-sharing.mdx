## Overview

You can share any file or folder with specific people by email. People in your workspace already have access to its files through their [workspace role](/account/members-and-roles), so sharing is mostly for people outside the workspace.

## Share a file or folder

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open sharing">
    Click **⋮** on the file or folder and choose **Share**. For the file or folder you have open, you can also click the **Share** button in the top toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add people">
    Type an email under **Invite with email address**, and choose a permission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust or remove access">
    Everyone who has access is listed below. Change someone's permission from the menu next to their name, or click **Delete** to remove their access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Permissions

| Permission | What they can do |
|------------|------------------|
| **Read** | View and download |
| **Write** | View and edit |
| **Full Access** | View, edit and manage the item |

People you share with see the items under **Shared** in their sidebar. They only see what you shared with them, not the rest of your workspace.

## Sharing through the agent

You can also ask the [agent](/agent/overview), for example "Share the Q4 report with sam@example.com so they can edit it." The agent shows exactly what it will share, and does it only after you allow it.
