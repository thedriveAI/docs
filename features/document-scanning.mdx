## Overview

The Drive AI [mobile app](/getting-started/mobile-app) turns your phone into a document scanner. You can scan in two places:

- **In a folder**, to save the scan as a PDF in that folder.
- **In the agent chat**, to hand the scan to the agent with a message, for example "File this receipt somewhere sensible."

## Before you start

- Install the app on iOS or Android and sign in. See [Get the app](#get-the-app).
- To save scans into a folder, you need to be able to add files to the workspace: owners, admins and members can, viewers can't. In a read-only team workspace, nobody can.
- Allow the app to use your camera when your phone asks.

## Scan into a folder

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the folder">
    In the app, go to the folder you want the scan in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the scan button">
    It's the document-scanner button at the bottom right, just above the **+** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the pages">
    Your phone's document scanner opens. Point the camera at the page. The scanner finds the edges of the page and crops it. Scan each page of the document in turn. On Android, one scan holds up to 20 pages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Finish in the scanner. The pages become one PDF, with one page per scan, and it uploads to the folder you have open.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* Screenshot: the mobile file browser showing the scan button above the + button */}

The PDF is named `Scan_` followed by the date and time, for example `Scan_2026-03-14T09-30-12.pdf`. The time is in UTC. To rename it, press and hold it and choose **Rename**.

A scan is an upload like any other:

- It counts toward your plan's upload limits and storage. See [Uploading files](/features/uploading-files#limits).
- A [workflow](/workflows/overview) with a **File Uploaded** trigger runs on it, so a workflow can read the scan and file it.
- Its text is read with OCR, up to your plan's number of scanned pages per file (5 on Free). See [What happens after upload](/features/uploading-files#what-happens-after-upload).

If something goes wrong, the app shows **Scan Failed** (the scanner couldn't make the PDF) or **Scan Upload Failed** (the upload didn't finish), with the reason.

## Scan in the agent chat

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the agent">
    Swipe left from your files, or tap the message bar at the bottom.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap + and choose Scan">
    Tap **+** next to the message box. Under **Add files**, choose **Scan**. **Photos** and **Files** are there too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan and send">
    Scan the pages as above. The scan is attached to your message as one PDF. Type what you want done with it and send.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A scan attached in the chat isn't saved to a folder. It belongs to the conversation, and the agent reads it from there. Ask the agent to save it if you want to keep it: it proposes where, and saves it once you allow it. Attachments you don't save are deleted after 30 days.

You can attach up to 10 files to a message, each up to 25 MB.

## Tips for better scans

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Good lighting" icon="sun">
    Scan in a well-lit place and avoid shadows across the page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A contrasting surface" icon="table">
    Lay the page flat on a surface of a different color, so the scanner can find its edges.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="One document per scan" icon="copy">
    Scan all the pages of one document in one go, so they end up in one PDF.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Long documents on a lower plan" icon="file-lines">
    On Free, only the first 5 scanned pages of each PDF are read. Split a long document into several scans, or upgrade, if you need the agent to read all of it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
