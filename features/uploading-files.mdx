## Ways to upload

### Drag and drop

Drag files or whole folders from your computer onto the file browser. When **Drop Files or Folders here** appears, let go. Folders keep their structure.

### The New button

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the folder">
    Go to the folder you want the files in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Upload">
    Click **New** in the toolbar and choose **Upload**. You can also right-click the file browser and choose **Upload Files**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick files or a folder">
    Drop files into the window, or click to browse. Choose **select a folder** to upload a whole folder. Use **Add more** to add files or folders, then click **Upload**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your plan sets how many files you can upload at once. If you pick more, the app tells you the limit.

### Other ways in

- **From Google Drive, OneDrive, SharePoint or Dropbox:** click **New** and choose **Import from Cloud**. See [Google Drive](/integrations/google-drive).
- **From your phone:** scan paper documents with the mobile app. See [Document scanning](/features/document-scanning).
- **From other people:** send a [file request](/features/file-requests).
- **From email, Slack and Teams:** set up a [workflow](/workflows/overview) that saves incoming files.
- **From the agent:** files the agent makes can be saved to a folder from the chat. See [The agent](/agent/overview#attaching-files-and-getting-files-back).

## What happens after upload

The Drive AI reads each file so you can search it and the agent can work with it:

- Text is extracted from documents, spreadsheets, presentations and PDFs.
- Scanned PDFs are read with OCR, up to your plan's OCR page allowance.
- Images are read, and audio and video are transcribed, on plans that include every file type (see [Plans and pricing](/account/plans-pricing)).

On the Free plan, and while a team workspace's subscription is paused or ended, images, audio and video are stored but not read.

Uploading files doesn't file them into folders by itself. To file uploads automatically, set up a [workflow](/workflows/overview) with a **File Uploaded** trigger. To file what's already in a folder, right-click and choose **Organize this folder (⌘K)**, or ask the [agent](/agent/overview).

## Storage

Each plan includes an amount of storage. See [Plans & Pricing](/account/plans-pricing). To see how much you've used, open **Settings** > **Usage** and look at **Storage**. Files in the Trash count toward storage until you delete them from there.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Upload failed" icon="circle-exclamation">
    - Check your internet connection.
    - Check you have storage left in **Settings** > **Usage**.
    - Try a smaller batch.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The agent can't read a file" icon="hourglass">
    - Large files take longer to process. Wait a few minutes.
    - Images, audio and video need a paid plan to be read.
    - Password-protected files can't be read. Remove the password and upload again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
